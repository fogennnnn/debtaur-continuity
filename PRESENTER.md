# Friday presenter runbook — both demos, one story

## Order (12 minutes)
1. **Guided run** — https://dem.oooooooooo.se/ — press Begin. Say nothing during
   the two long pauses; let the room read. Land the close on the buyer-or-lender line.
2. **Handover console** — https://testdeps2.fogeboro.workers.dev/ — First day tab.
   Walk Maya's morning (3 moments), then open the case queue and decide one case
   live with Alain's own numbers typed into the New case desk.
3. **The question** — "Which of your clients' processes breaks first when the
   manager leaves?" Encode their answer on the spot (Encode a policy tab) or
   promise it for next week.

## Timings
- Guided run: ~4 minutes with pauses. Never skip ahead; the locks are the point.
- First day: ~3 minutes. Queue demo: 3 minutes. Offer + questions: rest.

## Fallbacks
- No wifi: run `npm run demo` in debtaur-eos (port 8080) and debtaur-continuity
  (port 8081) from the laptop. Both work fully offline.
- Wrong answer on stage: every verdict shows the exact missing premise and who
  signs — read it aloud, it is always correct.
- Alain types hostile input: blanks and unknown values refuse by design.
  That IS the demo working.

## Lines that land
- "When a manager leaves, the rules stay."
- "Most businesses become less sellable the day a key person leaves. This is
  the counter-example."
- "After a PlanBot concept plan, this is the missing proof."
- Never: methodology names, engine-internals talk, or automation hype. The page contains none;
  keep it that way out loud.

## After Friday
- Real client numbers replace the samples (thresholds, signer titles, vendors).
  Swap = data edit + re-sign command in each README. Nothing else changes.
